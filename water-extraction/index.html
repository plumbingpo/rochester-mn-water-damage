Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • Store a bucket and spare towels where you can grab them, along with the number for a restoration company
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
