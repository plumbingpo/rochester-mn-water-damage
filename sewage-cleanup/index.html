Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Rochester Restoration Squad provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Rochester, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Rochester

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
