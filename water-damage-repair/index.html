What a Professional Brings to Water Damage Repair

Repair after water damage is not ordinary remodeling. Whoever rebuilds has to understand where the water travelled, what it affected and whether the framing inside the wall has fully dried. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. That history keeps problems from being covered over with new drywall and fresh paint.

Then there is the craft of making things match. A patch that shows is a repair you notice every day. Our finishers blend the texture on walls, carry paint to a natural stopping point and, where it exists, find flooring that aligns with your current boards. When a match is not possible, we lay out options, such as replacing flooring to a doorway. Our team pulls any permits the job needs and sends updates to your insurance company.

How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • No wall or floor should be sealed by any contractor until you have seen final moisture readings.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.

Signs You Need Water Damage Repair in Rochester

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Following water from above, part of the ceiling droops, has cracks or crumbles when touched.
  • Grout is breaking up and some tiles have lifted where moisture reached the floor below.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
