Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Rochester Restoration Squad handles emergency flood cleanup across Rochester and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Rochester

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Install a battery backup sump pump so you stay protected when storms knock out power
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
